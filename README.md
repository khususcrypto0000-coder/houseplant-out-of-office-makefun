Entry for [The Houseplant Out-of-Office](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-houseplant-out-of-office-7c586047) on make.fun — the funniest out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send, in 120 characters or fewer.

Repository: `houseplant-out-of-office-makefun`

## The entry

> Out of office: I'm your fern. Your watering performance: needs improvement. Review in spring. Bring water.

106 characters. The fern flips the office script: instead of begging for water, it puts its human on a performance review — and the human's watering has been rated "needs improvement".

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the single auto-reply to `/output/entry.txt`, with guards asserting it is 1–120 characters, a single line, and has no trailing newline.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
